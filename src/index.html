Whole-home audio, without the lock-in.

Music, art and light in microsecond sync. An open standard from the Open Home Foundation.

Try the live demo {% include "icons/arrow.svg" %} See how it works

Most standards stop at the sound.

Sendspin carries the artwork, the controls, and the beat alongside the audio. A speaker, a tablet, and a lamp all act as one room.

{% include "icons/speaker.svg" %}

Sound

High-quality audio, in sync across every speaker. Each one gets a stream matched to what it can play. Lossless to your hi-fi, efficient to the kitchen smart speaker, all locked to the same beat.

{% include "icons/monitor.svg" %}

Screens

Album art and now-playing info on every tablet and display. The hallway wall panel becomes a Now Playing screen. The kitchen shows what's on without anyone reaching for a phone.

{% include "icons/remote.svg" %}

Controls

Any screen becomes a remote. Skip, pause, change volume from anywhere in the house. Phone in your pocket or tablet on the wall, it's the same set of controls.

{% include "icons/light.svg" %}

Lights

LEDs and smart bulbs react to the music in real time. Bass on the floor lamp, mids on the strip behind the TV, in sync with what you're hearing.

One song. Every room. Locked to the same beat.

Microsecond-precise timing keeps every speaker in step. Walk between rooms and the music stays one song, not an echo chamber.

Your music,
in your home,
on your terms.

Sendspin doesn't lock you into a brand or a cloud. It's an open standard, run on your own network, governed by a non-profit. Nothing about your music leaves the house.

{% include "icons/branches.svg" %}

No brand lock-in.

The spec is public, so any maker can implement it: speakers, screens, or lights. The aim is a catalogue you pick from on how it sounds, not on the badge.

{% include "icons/handoff.svg" %}

Keep what you have.

Sendspin runs alongside AirPlay, so your existing speakers don't have to go in the bin. An old phone or a ten-dollar board brings the hi-fi in the attic back as another room.

{% include "icons/lock.svg" %}

No cloud account.

Everything stays on your home network. You never sign up for an account, and what you play stays between your own devices instead of going back to Big Tech.

{% include "icons/seed.svg" %}

Built to last.

Backed by the Open Home Foundation, a Swiss non-profit. Sendspin can't be bought out, paywalled, or shut down on a corporate whim.

Add rooms one at a time.

You need two things: a speaker, and something to source the music. Pick a path and add a single room. A new device shows up on your network, you approve it once, and it joins the rooms you already have. The rooms you set up earlier keep playing while you do it.
